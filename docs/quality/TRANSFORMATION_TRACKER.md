## Authority and evidence rules

Inputs reconciled here are the approved M0–M8 roadmap, the master engineering
directive (attachment `09355ff2-b595-449e-9357-7a56f8a8d787`, Parts 1–15), the
competitive/product discussion (`22e1165e-6962-4fa6-b209-ba1797ebfd73`), and the
active changes below. The later attachment `45e835a4-ed23-405e-b869-56ba100723df`
has identical bytes to the competitive discussion: SHA-256
`e389181f9705472f25f2c4a7610a0610f9c2d896bf7d2b571d6a62488c2829a7`.
The 2026-11-20 goal explicitly requires the whole program and ongoing work after
each increment. Earlier narrow change exclusions do not remove roadmap work.

Statuses mean:

- **Verified slice:** recorded checks support the named limited behavior; the
  broader row can still have open integration or qualification requirements.
- **Partial:** relevant source exists, but the complete acceptance contract is
  unfinished or evidence is insufficient.
- **Open:** implementation or its current evidence has not been established.
- **External gate:** physical hardware, credentials, controlled accounts,
  independent review or human participants are still needed. Implementable
  preparation and harness work remains ordinary open work.
- **Dependency gate:** the approved prerequisite has not passed; do not begin
  the gated capability merely because unrelated development is possible.

No status here is inferred from an OpenSpec checkbox alone. Source/test pointers
identify where to inspect and extend work; they do not assert that the tests
cover the entire row. The initial audit sampled browser execution most deeply.
Rows outside that slice still require dedicated source, threat and runtime
review. Amend rows when changes are integrated, with a dated evidence link and
actual commands/results. Keep failed attempts and unavailable cases visible.
