# Proposal

## Why

Managed-browser tasks can terminate on harmless observation failures, lose the distinction between a completed action and its failed follow-up read, and treat website-authored labels as proof that a click is reversible. The approved platform transformation starts by repairing these boundaries and making large, dynamic pages inspectable.

## What Changes

- Record the complete approved transformation program, its requirement owners, baseline evidence and outstanding qualification without replacing the maintained roadmap.
- Remove page-label-based reversible click classification; retain existing grants and non-bypassable restrictions.
- Introduce trusted browser failure stages, persisted error receipts, bounded safe recovery and successful-action receipts independent of follow-up observation.
- Add complete bounded page/control discovery and target-specific freshness while preserving exact observed identities, current authority and no replay of uncertain input.
- Consume action-returned observations without redundant model calls.
- Keep managed multi-origin/tab expansion, signed-in extension attachment, other inference providers, image generation and Anthropic transport as subsequent independently reviewable increments of the approved program. This change does not certify or remove those requirements.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `browser-execution`: policy, observable effect boundaries, recovery and complete bounded discovery for managed browser actions.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing native authority, task completion and proposal repair contracts remain enforced.

## Impact

Browser companion, browser/service transport, durable agent recovery, related tests and maintained quality/browser documentation. Additive negotiated discovery contracts preserve older companions. No dependencies, automatic downloads, installed-user changes, commits or publication are required.
